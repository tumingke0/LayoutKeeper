# LayoutKeeper 0.6.12

## 改动
- **按机身接口自动切换显示器排列**：同一块显示器插在左边 USB-C 口时放在旁边、插在右边 USB-C 口或 HDMI 口时放在上方的话，现在可以在「菜单栏 → 显示器排列」或「设置 → 显示器」里点「插在 USB-C 口 #1 时自动用…」。下次插到对应接口时会自动切换排列，且不会触发多余的窗口恢复（[issue #1](https://github.com/tumingke0/LayoutKeeper/issues/1)）。
- **防休眠（Keep Awake）**：菜单栏与「设置 → 通用」新增「防休眠」，可选「息屏不休眠（允许黑屏，合盖自动保活）」或「常亮不休眠」。解决开启了 macOS「显示器关闭时防止自动睡眠」后，在电池供电、插电源线短暂唤醒（Notification Wake）或合盖外接屏黑屏（Clamshell Sleep）时仍然休眠断网的问题，保持 Codex、SSH 和远程连接不断线。
- **区分序列号相同的同型号显示器**：部分显示器（如部分三星、LG 型号）上报的数字序列号是固定占位值，现在会自动回退读取 EDID 里的字母序列号来区分，已有正常序列号的显示器不受影响。
- **连续自动补桌面更稳**：一次补多个桌面时，等待调度中心完成前一个桌面的动画再按「+」，不会再因为界面短暂刷新而中途停在第一个桌面。
- **合盖期间新建的桌面也会记下**：合上笔记本盖子使用外接屏时，在调度中心新建的桌面和上面的窗口现在会正常纳入记录。
- **锁屏与息屏时不再空跑扫描**：锁屏、屏幕休眠或刚唤醒的前几秒直接跳过后台窗口扫描；自动恢复发现所有开着的窗口都已在原位时，不再因为旧布局里已关闭的窗口弹出常驻通知。

0.6.1 及以上：菜单栏 → **检查更新…**，辅助功能权限保持有效。

## English
- **Switch display arrangement by physical Mac port**: if the same monitor sits beside your MacBook on one USB-C port and above it on another USB-C or HDMI port, bind the current arrangement to that port in **Menu Bar → Display Arrangement** or **Settings → Displays**. Next time you plug the monitor into that port, LayoutKeeper switches the arrangement automatically before restoring windows ([issue #1](https://github.com/tumingke0/LayoutKeeper/issues/1)).
- **Keep Awake**: new **Keep Awake** option in the menu bar and **Settings → General**, with **Keep System Awake (Allow Screen Off; Clamshell Safe)** and **Keep Screen & System Awake**. Prevents macOS from still going to sleep on battery power, after a cable-plug Notification Wake, or 5 seconds after the external display turns off with the lid closed (Clamshell Sleep), keeping Codex, SSH, and remote sessions connected.
- **Tells apart same-model monitors with placeholder numeric serials**: when a monitor reports `0` or `0x01010101` as its numeric serial number, LayoutKeeper falls back to the alphanumeric serial in its EDID `0xFF` descriptor. Existing monitors with real numeric serials keep their layout keys unchanged.
- **More reliable multi-desktop auto-add**: waits for Mission Control to finish rebuilding its accessibility tree between consecutive `+` clicks when adding several missing desktops in a row.
- **Keeps new desktops created in clamshell mode**: desktops added on the external monitor while the MacBook lid is closed are now appended to the desk strip and their windows are recorded.
- **Skips background scans while locked or asleep**: no longer runs timed-out AX scans while the screen is locked or asleep, and automatic restore stays quiet when all open windows are already in place.
- **Updating**: from 0.6.1 or later use **Check for Updates…**; Accessibility permission is kept. Self-signed, not notarized: right-click → Open on first launch.
