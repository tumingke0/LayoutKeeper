# LayoutKeeper 0.6.10

## 改动
- **显示器排列**：同一块外接屏有时放在 MacBook 上方、有时放在旁边的话，现在不用再去系统设置里拖了。菜单栏 →「显示器排列」里直接选「外接屏在正上方 / 正右方 / 正下方 / 正左方」，当前所在的那一边会打勾。这个需求来自 [issue #1](https://github.com/tumingke0/LayoutKeeper/issues/1)。
- **切换后 15 秒内确认**：切换后屏幕上方会出现「保留这个排列？」，15 秒内点「保留」才会留下，不点、按 Esc 或点左上角的 × 都会变回原来的排列。退出 LayoutKeeper 或 Mac 睡眠时，没确认的切换也会自动还原。
- **自定义位置**：正上、正右、正下、正左以外的摆法（比如靠左、错开一点），可以在 设置 → 显示器 里选好边和对齐方式，有示意图可看，然后存成自定义位置，之后在菜单里一键切换，也可以给它设快捷键。分辨率变了也会按原来的相对位置重新排。
- 只改屏幕之间的相对位置：主屏幕和分辨率不变，切换时不会触发窗口恢复。
- 按接口（比如左边、右边的 USB-C 口）自动切换排列还没做。

## 更新
0.6.1 及以上：菜单栏 → **检查更新…**，辅助功能权限保持有效。

## English
- **Display arrangement**: if the same monitor sometimes sits above your MacBook and sometimes beside it, you no longer need to drag it around in System Settings. In the menu bar, Display Arrangement now offers **Monitor directly above / right / below / left**, with a check mark on the side it's on now. Requested in [issue #1](https://github.com/tumingke0/LayoutKeeper/issues/1).
- **15 seconds to confirm**: after a switch, "Keep this arrangement?" appears at the top of the screen. The new arrangement stays only if you click **Keep** within 15 seconds; doing nothing, pressing Esc or clicking the × in the top-left corner switches back. An unconfirmed switch is also undone when LayoutKeeper quits or the Mac goes to sleep.
- **Custom positions**: for anything other than the four centred positions (left-aligned, shifted a little…), choose the side and alignment in Settings → Displays, with a small diagram, and save it as a custom position. It then appears in the menu and can have its own shortcut. If a resolution changes, the displays are re-placed using the same relative position.
- Only the displays' relative position changes: the main display and the resolution stay the same, and switching never triggers a window restore.
- Switching automatically by which port (for example the left or right USB-C port) the monitor is plugged into isn't done yet.
- **Updating**: from 0.6.1 or later use **Check for Updates…**; Accessibility permission is kept. Self-signed, not notarized: right-click → Open on first launch.
