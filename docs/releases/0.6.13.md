# LayoutKeeper 0.6.13

## 改动
- **防休眠菜单增加直观的休眠条件说明**：在菜单栏「防休眠」子菜单和「设置 → 通用 → 防休眠」中，每档下方都直接标明开盖、合盖、拔掉电源或外接屏时的具体休眠表现，一眼就能看清区别。
- **新增「合盖断电也不休眠」选项**：开启后，即使合上 MacBook 盖子并拔掉电源线和外接显示器，系统也会继续保持运行不断网（首次开启需管理员授权一次，后续切换免密；装进包里前请记得关闭，避免在包内发热耗电）。切回其他模式或退出 LayoutKeeper 时会自动恢复系统正常睡眠设置。

## 更新
0.6.1 及以上：菜单栏 → **检查更新…**，辅助功能权限保持有效。

## English
- **Clear sleep-condition subtitles in Keep Awake**: each Keep Awake mode in the menu bar and **Settings → General → Keep Awake** now shows a concise subtitle explaining when the Mac sleeps or stays awake (lid open vs. closed, on battery vs. power/monitor).
- **New "Keep Awake Even Closed on Battery" mode**: keeps the Mac running even when you close the lid on battery power with no external monitor connected (asks for administrator permission once on first use, then switches without prompting; turn it off before putting the Mac in a bag). Switching to another mode or quitting LayoutKeeper automatically restores normal system sleep.
- **Updating**: from 0.6.1 or later use **Check for Updates…**; Accessibility permission is kept. Self-signed, not notarized: right-click → Open on first launch.
